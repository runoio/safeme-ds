**IconButton** — a compact button wrapping one icon, for headers, toolbars and list rows. Always pass `aria-label`.

```jsx
<IconButton variant="safe" aria-label="Zadzwoń"><Phone size={20} /></IconButton>
<IconButton variant="ghost" aria-label="Ustawienia"><Settings size={20} /></IconButton>
```

Props: `variant` (solid | safe | ghost | outline), `size` (sm | md | lg), `round`.
